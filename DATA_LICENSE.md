# Data license

The aggregated counts in this repository were compiled by the authors from
administrative records held by the admission agencies listed in the README.
They are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Cite the paper (see `CITATION.cff`) when you use them.

The license covers:

- `07_revision/02_data/harmonized/master/` and `07_revision/02_data/harmonized/cache/`
- `07_revision/02_data/harmonized/figures/*.csv` and `figures/data/`

Two small third-party series are included only because the code reads them.
They remain under their publishers' terms:

- `07_revision/02_data/gender_wage_gap_10countries_mixed_sources.csv`: gender
  wage gaps compiled from ILOSTAT and national statistical offices; each row
  names its source and URL.
- `05_data/wef_gender_gap_2025_sample.csv`: World Economic Forum, *Global
  Gender Gap Report 2025*, index values for the ten countries.

The individual-level records behind the counts are not included and cannot be
redistributed. The README says whom to ask for each setting.
